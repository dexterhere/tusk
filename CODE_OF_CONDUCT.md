# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept specific, constructive feedback. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy: never post others' personal data, and never post secrets or customer data.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome attention.
- Doxxing, or publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict or ban people who break these rules. Report problems privately through the repository's private reporting channel (Security tab) or by contacting a maintainer directly; reports are kept confidential and reviewed fairly.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
